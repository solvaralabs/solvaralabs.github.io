FAQ

Before you buy

Will these tools make me money?

They are tools, not a promise. RugScope helps you avoid bad tokens, FastSwap executes your decisions faster and cheaper, and the AI Meme Trader is an experiment with strict risk limits. Meme coins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Never trade money you can't afford to lose.

Do you ever hold my wallet or keys?

No. Everything runs on your server. The wallet key is a file you create, and it signs transactions locally. The code doesn't send data to us.

Why one-time pricing instead of a subscription?

You run it on your own server with your own API keys, so there is nothing for us to host. You pay once and own your copy.

How does FastSwap compare to hosted trading bots?

Hosted bots usually take about 1% of every trade and hold your wallet for you. FastSwap takes nothing and your key stays on your machine. At $5,000 of monthly volume, 1% is $50 a month.

I'm not very technical. Can I still set it up?

If you can follow a step-by-step guide and paste commands into a terminal, yes. Each product has a doctor command that tells you exactly which key or setting is missing, and setup help is included for 30 days.

Can I resell the code?

No. The license covers your own use, including running the bot for your own community. Reselling or publishing the code isn't allowed.

A prepaid 30-day pass for our hosted scanner bot, for people who want rug checks without running anything. It doesn't renew by itself: you get a reminder before it ends. Want unlimited checks for your own community, with your own branding? That's RugScope AI.
